# Paper authority and working rules

The authoritative Paper masters are **Master · Desktop · Light & Dark** ([p-T-1](https://app.paper.design/file/01M2TMFZ6NBQ22TWSCHRJK9JPP/p-T-1)) and **Master · Mobile · Light & Dark** ([p-U-1](https://app.paper.design/file/01M2TMFZ6NBQ22TWSCHRJK9JPP/p-U-1)) in the existing Paper document **USA X — 2D redesign directions** (`01M2TMFZ6NBQ22TWSCHRJK9JPP`). They define the interface composition, controls, imagery, density, theme treatment, and states. Inspect the current native master state and layer structure before Paper work; dated exports and runtime screenshots provide context but do not replace the current source. A later explicit owner decision may update a master.

## Preserve the masters

Start each iteration from the complete matching master state. Preserve composition, navigation, panels, typography, spacing, and interactions unless the active task authorizes a change. Duplicate the relevant complete frame to a separate working page, record the exact source frame and mapping, and retain the source master and comment anchors. Compare the result beside its exact source state, including navigation, selection, data and legend, advertising, sources, fixed controls, safe area, theme, and the feature under review. Keep alternatives on working pages; promote only after review and explicit owner authorization.

The desktop master includes Map / Compare / Stats / Blog, separate Support and Info controls, a 2D/3D control, indicator/place rail, selected-place content, quick statistics, advertising, attribution, and sources. Preserve first-open and selected-place differences. The mobile master uses state-specific bottom lanes: first-open map navigation; selected-state sheet with contextual Compare; and a separate statistics page with inline advertising. Do not impose the four-tab lane on every screen. Derive county states from the matching approved place-detail pattern while retaining exact geography and context.

The 22 October AI assistant studies on Paper pages `p-13-0`, `p-15-0`, and `p-14-0` are review history, not approved sources. Their earlier design-fidelity acceptance was withdrawn because they rebuilt the shell and failed to preserve the masters' composition and functions. Rework from the masters; do not treat this correction as backend acceptance or master promotion.

For delegated Paper work, provide both master links, exact frame references, preservation requirements, and the expected source comparison. Primary acceptance includes the master comparison and the requested feature.
